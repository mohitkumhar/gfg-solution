<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/polynomial-addition/1">Polynomial Addition</a></h2><h3>Medium</h3><hr><p data-path-to-node="8,0"><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Given two polynomials represented by linked lists, add them by summing the coefficients of terms with the same power. The lists are sorted in descending order of powers.</span></p>
<p data-path-to-node="8,0"><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Structure of the linked list:</span></p>
<p data-path-to-node="8,0"><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><img src="https://media.geeksforgeeks.org/img-practice/prod/addEditProblem/928183/Web/Other/blobid9_1781524170.png" height="100"></span></p>
<p data-path-to-node="8,0"><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong data-path-to-node="8,1" data-index-in-node="0">Note:</strong> If the sum of coefficients for a specific power becomes zero, that term should not be included in the resulting linked list.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>head1 = [1, 3] , head2 = [1, 3]<strong><br></strong><img src="https://media.geeksforgeeks.org/img-practice/prod/addEditProblem/928183/Web/Other/blobid0_1781522856.png" width="236" height="48"> &nbsp;  <img src="https://media.geeksforgeeks.org/img-practice/prod/addEditProblem/928183/Web/Other/blobid1_1781522916.png" width="229" height="48"><br><strong>Output: </strong>head = [1, 3] -&gt; [1, 2]<br><img src="https://media.geeksforgeeks.org/img-practice/prod/addEditProblem/928183/Web/Other/blobid4_1781523692.png" width="343" height="56">
<strong>Explanation: </strong>head1 represents x<sup>3</sup>, head2 represents x<sup>2</sup>, add both the polynomials to get x<sup>3</sup> + x<sup>2</sup>.</span>
</pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>head1 = [1, 3] -&gt; [2, 2], head2 = [3, 3] -&gt; [4, 2]<br><img src="https://media.geeksforgeeks.org/img-practice/prod/addEditProblem/928183/Web/Other/blobid2_1781523106.png" width="343" height="56"> &nbsp; <img src="https://media.geeksforgeeks.org/img-practice/prod/addEditProblem/928183/Web/Other/blobid3_1781523159.png" width="343" height="56"><br><strong>Output: </strong>head = [4, 3] -&gt; [6, 2]<br><img src="https://media.geeksforgeeks.org/img-practice/prod/addEditProblem/928183/Web/Other/blobid5_1781523715.png" width="343" height="56"> <br><strong>Explanation: </strong>head1 represents x<sup>3 </sup>+ 2x<sup>2</sup>, head2 represents 3x<sup>3</sup> + 4x<sup>2</sup>. </span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Since, x<sup>3</sup></span><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-size: 14pt;"> </span>has two different coefficients as 3 and 1. Adding them up will lead to 4x<sup>3</sup></span><span style="font-size: 18px;">. Also, x<sup>2</sup></span><span style="font-size: 18px;"> has two coefficients as 4 and 2. So, adding them up will give 6x<sup>2</sup></span><span style="font-size: 18px;">.</span></pre>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Constraints:</strong><br>1&nbsp;<span style="font-size: 14pt;">≤</span> no. of nodes in head1, head2 <span style="font-size: 14pt;">≤</span>&nbsp;10<sup>5</sup><br>1&nbsp;<span style="font-size: 14pt;">≤</span>&nbsp;node-&gt;data&nbsp;<span style="font-size: 14pt;">≤</span>&nbsp;10<sup>6</sup></span></p>

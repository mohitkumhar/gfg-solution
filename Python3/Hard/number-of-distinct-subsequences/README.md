<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/number-of-distinct-subsequences0909/1">Number of distinct subsequences</a></h2><h3>Hard</h3><hr><p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given a string <strong>str</strong> consisting of lowercase english alphabets, find the<strong> </strong>number of distinct subsequences of the string</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Note:</strong> Answer can be very large, so, return answer modulo 10<sup>9</sup>+7.</span></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">Examples:</span></strong></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input:</strong> str = "gfg"</span>
<span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Output:</strong> 7
<strong>Explanation:</strong> <br>The seven distinct subsequences are "", "g", "f", "gf", "fg", "gg" and "gfg" .</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input:</strong> str = "ggg"</span>
<span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Output:</strong> 4
<strong>Explanation:</strong> <br>The four distinct subsequences are "", "g", "gg", "ggg".</span></pre>

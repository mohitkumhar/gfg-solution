<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/minimum-cost-to-visit-favorite-cities--132208/1">Minimum cost to visit favorite Colonies</a></h2><h3>Hard</h3><hr><p><span style="font-size: 18.6667px;">There are <strong>n</strong> colonies in GeekCity, numbered from 1 to n. Geek has a set of favorite colonies <strong>favorite[]</strong> and he needs to visit all of these colonies.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18.6667px;">Given a 2D integer array <strong>roads[][]</strong> of size n-1, where roads[i] = [a, b] represents a road that connects two colonies a and b. The road network is designed in such a way that there is a unique path between any two colonies, ensuring there are no loops or multiple routes between the colonies.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18.6667px;">The cost of moving between any two colonies directly connected by a road is the absolute difference of their colony numbers. If a road is used more than once, its cost is counted only the first time it is traversed. Find the <strong>minimum</strong> total cost that Geek needs to spend to visit all of his favorite[] colonies at least once, starting from any colony.</span></p>
<p><strong style="font-size: 14pt;">Examples:</strong></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input: </strong>roads[][] = [[1, 2], [2, 3], [3, 4], [3, 5]], favorites[] = [1, 4, 5]<br><strong>Output: </strong>5<br><strong>Explanation: </strong>Geek can move in the city in the order, 1 -&gt; 2 -&gt; 3 -&gt; 4 -&gt; 3 -&gt; 5. Total cost he needs to spend will be (1 + 1 + 1 + 2) i.e. 5.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input: </strong>roads[][] = [[1, 2], [2, 3], [2, 4], [4, 5]], favorites[] = [2, 5]<br><strong>Output: </strong>3<br><strong>Explanation: </strong>Geek can move in the city in the order, 2 -&gt; 4 -&gt; 5. Total cost he needs to spend will be (2 + 1) i.e. 3.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input: </strong>roads[][] = [[1, 2], [1, 3], [1, 4]], favorites[] = [1]<br><strong>Output: </strong>0<br><strong>Explanation: </strong>Geek can visit only colony 1. Total cost he needs to spend will be 0.</span></pre>
